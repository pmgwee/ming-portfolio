## Product Principles
1. Show a specific task and inspectable result before asking visitors to read architecture.
2. Attribute each contribution and distinguish running systems, prototypes, open pull requests, and illustrative examples.
3. Keep detailed evidence one step away from a concise first explanation.
4. Give recruiters and studio clients routes that remain understandable throughout the page.
5. Preserve creative identity while keeping core information available without animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
The portfolio should work with keyboard navigation and assistive technologies, respect reduced-motion preferences, and remain readable on mobile without relying on hover or animation to convey essential information.
